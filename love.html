From my heart

A letter for you

My love,

Today isn't just another birthday. Today is the day the world gets to celebrate you — the person who somehow managed to become such an important part of my life.

I honestly don't know how to fit everything I feel into a few paragraphs. There are so many little things about you that I could talk about forever. The way you laugh. The way you talk about the things you love. The little moments that probably mean nothing to anyone else but somehow mean everything to me.

You have this beautiful way of making ordinary days feel special. A conversation with you can turn a boring day into one I'll remember. And sometimes I catch myself smiling just because I remembered something you said.

I hope you know that you are appreciated. Not just today because it's your birthday, but on all the random ordinary days too.

Now you're stepping into eighteen. A whole new chapter is waiting for you, and I hope it's filled with everything your heart has been wishing for.

I hope you chase your dreams without being afraid of how big they are. I hope you laugh until your stomach hurts, discover places you've never seen, meet people who appreciate you, and make memories that you'll still smile about years from now.

And selfishly... I hope I get to be there for some of those memories.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every little moment, and every memory we've created.and every spicy moment 😏.

If I could give you one thing for your birthday, it wouldn't be something wrapped in a box. I'd give you the ability to see yourself through the eyes of everyone who cares about you — because then you'd understand just how special you are.

Happy 18th birthday, beautiful.

Here's to you, to this new chapter, and to all the beautiful moments still waiting somewhere ahead of us.

Always cheering for you,
Gabrielle ❤️
